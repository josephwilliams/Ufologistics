# Level 1 Tutorial Arc — After Roswell (8 beats)

Goal: ramp the player from **two-click route creation** to the full toolset —
**contract board + route inspector + first mitigation tool** — without a wall of UI.
One new system per beat; each beat unlocks the next.

| # | Player action | System taught | Narrative wrapper | Failure condition | Promotion / unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Landfall** | Click the pulsing Roswell base | Globe, zoom, your hub | You drop into the still-smoldering crash cover. The last operator is gone; the lid is yours now. | — (cannot fail) | First saucer fielded |
| **2 · First Line** | Wire Ranch α → Roswell (two clicks) | Route creation, auto-shuttle, currency trickle | "Rebuild what your predecessor ran — quietly." | — | **Contract Board** unlocks |
| **3 · Take a Contract** | Accept the Grey "Milk Run" from the board | Contracts as *goals* (not free-play), payouts | The Greys remind you that you serve patrons, not yourself. | Let the contract expire → minor Standing loss | 2nd source site + 2nd route slot |
| **4 · Read the Inspector** | Click a route → open the breakdown | The craft × crew × corridor stat model (read-only) | "Every run leaves a trace. Learn to read yours." | — | **Corridor choice** unlocks |
| **5 · Choose a Corridor** | Re-route a line via Night Run / Desert Dogleg | Corridor doctrine = exposure/speed tradeoff | A rancher photographed your last run. Fly smarter, not straighter. | Keep Direct Line in daylight → Exposure climbs to a warning | **Crew assignment** unlocks |
| **6 · Assign Crew** | Swap Grey Drones → Tall Grey on a hot route | Crew layer (cost vs exposure) | Adult supervision arrives from the home office. | All-drone sprawl pushes Exposure past 60% (yellow) | **First mitigation tool: Men-in-Black** |
| **7 · Mitigate** | Dispatch MiB to a witness/hot site to scrub heat | *Active* exposure management (not just avoidance) | A man in a black suit knocks on a door. The story changes by morning. | Ignore a witness event → Disclosure +15 | A **Nordic _or_ Mantid** contract appears (first taste of the alignment fork) |
| **8 · The Squeeze** | Fulfill a deadline+exposure contract (Mantid "Cold Samples", decaying) using everything | **Synthesis** — board + inspector + corridor + crew + mitigation at once | "Prove you're not the last guy." The Federation is watching the renewal. | Miss the deadline **or** trip Disclosure → restart the beat | **License Renewal passes → promoted to Crew Chief.** Level 2 (Continental) unlocks: Element-115 fuel, orbital reach |

## Pacing notes

- **Beats 1–3** are the hand-holdy core (≤ 10 min): the player should bank their first contract
  payout fast — that's the dopamine hook.
- **Beats 4–6** introduce the *depth* one knob at a time (read → corridor → crew), so the route
  inspector never dumps three systems at once.
- **Beat 7** is the pivot from *avoiding* exposure to *managing* it — the conceptual heart of the
  game, and the moment the alignment fork is first teased.
- **Beat 8** removes the rails: a real deadline under real exposure pressure, judged by the
  Federation. Passing it *is* the Level 1 win and the bridge to Level 2's wider, less-guided map
  (see the level openness curve in `SPIKE.md`).
