# Level 1 — Americas Site List

Canonical site table for the Level 1 (Americas) theatre. Coordinates align with the prototype's
in-code `SITES`. Types: **base** (hub/port) · **lab** (process/unlock) · **harvest** (source) ·
**attraction** (tourism source) · **hazard** (risk/opportunity terrain).

> **Era note (1947–1965):** Varginha (1996) and Buga (2025) are lore-compressed — treat them as the
> *modern names* for Brazil/Colombia hot zones that are already active in-era (Villas-Bôas 1957,
> Trindade 1958 stand in narratively). Everything else is era-native.
