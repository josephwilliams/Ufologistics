# Level 1 — Americas Contract Board (1947–1965)

20 contracts, 4 per patron. Currencies: Grey **Biomass ≋** · Nordic **Footage ◉** · Mantid
**Genome ✶** · Reptilian **Tribute ▼** · Federation **Standing ✦**. Exposure pressure is the
per-run heat; time pressure is the deadline; the wrinkle is the one twist that makes it a story.

> Era-correct event flavor leans on real 1947–65 cases (Roswell '47, Flight 19 '45, Mantell '48,
> Lubbock Lights '51, Washington flap '52, Kelly–Hopkinsville '55, Levelland '57, Villas-Bôas '57,
> Trindade '58, Socorro '64).
